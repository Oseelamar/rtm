Questions

What people ask us first.

How much will I have to pay?

Nothing up front. Our no-fee guarantee means you only pay if we win your case. Any fees come from the compensation we recover for you.

Will you help me get treatment?

Yes, even if you don’t have insurance. We work with a network of providers who can treat you now, with payment deferred until your case is resolved.

How long will my case take?

Every case is different, and timelines vary with your situation. We work to resolve your case as quickly as possible while getting the best outcome, and we keep you updated every step of the way.

How should I prepare?

Focus on taking care of yourself, and gather any documents you think may matter, like medical records or accident reports. We’ll handle the legal side while you recover.

When can I get back to my old life?

No one can say exactly when life will return to normal. We’re committed to helping you through it and securing the money you need to rebuild.
